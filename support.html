Onsite Support

Short daily practice for coding interviews, on iPhone.

Need help? Email matthew.alunni@gmail.com and say what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you're on. You'll usually get a reply within two working days.

Onsite Pro

The first units of the coding path are free. Onsite Pro opens the rest of the coding path, ladders, system design and your readiness score. It's available monthly or yearly, and the yearly plan may include a free week for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account.

Restore a purchase

Already subscribed, but Onsite shows padlocks, for example on a new iPhone or after reinstalling? Open Settings in the app (the gear on the Learn tab) and tap Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore at the bottom of the Pro screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change your plan

On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Onsite. You can also get there from Settings in the app. If you cancel, Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your own drill packs

Drill packs are JSON files. With iCloud Drive turned on, open the Files app and go to iCloud Drive › Onsite › Packs, then drop a pack there. It appears on your path next to the built-in lessons. If a pack doesn't show up, check that iCloud Drive is on for Onsite and that the file ends in .json.

Your progress

Progress is stored on your iPhone, and there's no account. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Restoring your iPhone from a backup brings it back.
