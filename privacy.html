Onsite Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version: Onsite has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves it is what's needed to sell and check an Onsite Pro subscription, handled by Apple and our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

This policy covers the Onsite app for iPhone ("Onsite", "the app"), made by Matthew Alunni ("we", "us"). It explains what information the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have.

Information that stays on your device

We don't receive any of the following. It's stored only on your iPhone, inside the app:

  • Your learning progress: lessons you've finished, review cards and their schedule, and your session history.
  • Your settings and onboarding answers: when your interview is, your target level, your preferred coding language and your daily goal.

Deleting the app deletes this data. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, the backup may include it, under Apple's terms and your own backup settings.

Personal packs in iCloud Drive

If you use iCloud Drive, Onsite creates a Packs folder in its iCloud Drive folder and reads any practice packs you put there. Those files live in your own iCloud account, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't access them.

Subscriptions and purchases

Onsite Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, email address, Apple Account or payment details.

To check whether you have Onsite Pro, including after a reinstall or on another device, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service that acts on our behalf. When the app opens and when you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives:

  • A random anonymous ID that the app creates. It isn't linked to your name, email address or Apple Account.
  • Your App Store purchase and subscription records for Onsite: which plan, when it started or renews, and any trial.
  • Basic technical details: app version, iOS version, device model, language and region, and the IP address the request comes from.

This is used only to deliver and support your subscription, for example turning Pro on, restoring a purchase, or handling a refund, and to see in aggregate how subscriptions are doing. It isn't used for advertising or sold to anyone.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no request for the advertising identifier.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Apple may share crash reports with us if you've turned on "Share with App Developers" in your iPhone's settings.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete the app. RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete them; see below.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your on-device data: delete the Onsite app.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription: go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Access or delete subscription records: email us at matthew.alunni@gmail.com. Because the ID is anonymous, we may ask for details such as when you subscribed, or your App Store order ID from the receipt Apple emailed you, so we can find your records.

Depending on where you live, for example the EU, UK or California, you may have more rights, such as access, correction, deletion and portability, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We honour these requests for everyone, wherever they live.

Children

Onsite is made for people preparing for technical job interviews and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, for example adding analytics, we'll also say so in the app's release notes.
